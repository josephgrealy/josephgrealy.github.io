This personal project started from being bored on the tube. I started logging all of my journeys on the Victoria line using Google Forms and then built an R Shiny dashboard to visualise the data. I collected a range of metrics about each journey and turned them into different charts and summaries. There is no particularly advanced stats, but maybe one of my favourite projects! I am now rebuilding the idea as a proper native iOS app in Swift, so there is more to come.

[View the dashboard](https://josephgrealy.shinyapps.io/tube-dashboard/)
