One of my main Defra projects was looking at uncertainty in air quality and emissions modelling. The policy questions were often quite simple — for example, how likely are we to meet a particular emissions target? — but the models underneath them were much more complicated, and uncertainty had not always been quantified or communicated consistently. I worked on methods including Monte Carlo simulation, correlated error propagation, FAST sensitivity analysis and Gaussian Process emulation, using R to build reproducible workflows and interactive outputs. I also spent a lot of time thinking about how to explain uncertainty clearly, rather than leaving it buried in technical documentation. The work fed into dashboards, Official Statistics and policy analysis used for target setting and international negotiations.
